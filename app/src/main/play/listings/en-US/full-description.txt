Keep your Wi-Fi connection details right where you need them: on your home screen.

WiFi Widget is a free, open-source Android app that displays detailed Wi-Fi information in a customizable home screen widget. Choose what to show, arrange it your way, and check or copy connection details directly in the app.

<b>Wi-Fi and network details</b>
• Connection: SSID, BSSID, frequency, channel, link speed, RSSI, signal strength, Wi-Fi standard, generation and security protocol
• IP addresses: local and public addresses, IPv4 and IPv6, prefix lengths and IPv4 subnet masks
• Network configuration: gateway, DNS servers, DHCP and NAT64 prefix
• Internet details: ISP, ASN and approximate IP-based location
• Optional GPS location information

<b>Customize your home screen widget</b>
• Select the properties you want to display and reorder them
• Choose light or dark themes, dynamic or custom colors, and background opacity
• Adjust font size, text alignment and widget size
• Add buttons to refresh data, open Wi-Fi settings or configure the widget
• Set automatic refresh intervals from 15 minutes to 24 hours, including low-battery behavior

<b>Useful beyond the home screen</b>
• View your current Wi-Fi status and connection details in the app
• Tap property values to copy them to the clipboard
• Enjoy a Material 3 interface with light, dark and AMOLED black themes
• No ads

Some information requires internet access or Android location permissions. Available properties may vary by device and Android version.
